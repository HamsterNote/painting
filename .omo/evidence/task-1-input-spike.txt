Decision
- Selected path: replace input internals with Pointer Events.
- Implementation path: `packages/painting/src/input/pointerInputController.ts` is the thin internal controller stub for Task 13 gesture work. `DrawingSurface` creates it with `gesturesEnabled: false` and still preserves existing public exports from `packages/painting/src/index.ts`.

Why not reuse @system-ui-js/multi-drag for gestures
- Current `packages/painting/src/multi-drag.d.ts` only describes `Drag`, `DragOperationType.Move`, `DragOperationType.AllEnd`, options, and `Finger.getPath()`.
- Installed `node_modules/@system-ui-js/multi-drag/dist/drag/finger.d.ts` exposes `Finger.pointerId`, but `node_modules/@system-ui-js/multi-drag/dist/drag/drag.js` constructs `DragBase` with `{ ...options, maxFingerCount: -1 }`, overriding caller `maxFingerCount: 1`.
- Installed `DragBase` mutates pose during pointer move via `setPose`, while drawing needs default-off gesture behavior and one-pointer drawing unchanged.
